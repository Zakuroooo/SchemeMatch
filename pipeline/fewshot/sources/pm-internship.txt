Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS) aims to provide internship opportunities to youth in government departments, public sector organizations, and reputed institutions for skill development and work experience.
Benefits Offered Under the Scheme
Practical exposure to real work environment
Skill development and hands-on experience
Opportunity to work with government/public sector
Enhances employability and career prospects
Certification on completion
Exposure to policy-making and administration
Eligibility
Students and young professionals
Pursuing or completed graduation/post-graduation
Meeting criteria specified by respective departments
Indian citizens
Application Details
Apply through official internship portals/notifications
Submit application form and required documents
Selection based on eligibility and merit
Internship duration as specified
Certification after completion
