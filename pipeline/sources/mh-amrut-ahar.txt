### Beneficiaries

All pregnant and lactating mothers and children aged 7 months to 6 years residing in Anganwadi/Mini Anganwadi areas of scheduled area.

### About the scheme: -

Bharat ratna Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Amrut Aahar Yojana was started to reduce malnutrition, Low Birth Weight (LBW) and Anemia amongst pregnant/lactating mothers besides aiding increase in BMI of mothers in Tribal Areas.

### Objectives

*   To take care of the health of pregnant women and lactating mothers in the scheduled area.
*   Reduce the rate of low birth weight babies
*   Eradicate malnutrition.
*   To reduce the rate of anemia in women and children
*   Under phase 2 children aged 7 months to 6 years will get Rs.4 days in a week. 6/- Banana / Eggs are provided within the price limit.

### Details

Food served under the Amrut Aahaar Yojana:

### **For Children:**

*   1 Egg / 1 Banana per day

### **For Pregnant Women /Lactating Mothers:**

*   Chapati/Bhakri
*   Rice
*   Daal
*   Green Vegetables 
*   2 Eggs / 2 Bananas
*   Peanut Laddo/Soya Milk

The scheme is implemented through 14322 Anganwadis in 111 projects in 16 districts of the state. Under this scheme under phase 1 in this area, pregnant women and lactating mothers will get Rs. 45/- one time full meal is provided within this expenditure limit.

Under phase 2 children aged 7 months to 6 years will get Rs.4 days in a week. 6/- Banana / Eggs are provided within the price limit.
