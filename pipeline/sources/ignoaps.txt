# Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme

**Date :** 01/01/2000 - 

**Portal** : [https://nsap.nic.in/](https://nsap.nic.in/)

**Eligibility Criteria**

Eligible elderly individuals aged 65 and above, whose names are on the Below Poverty Line (BPL) list, receive monthly financial assistance under the Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme.

**Nature of Benefits:**

From the central government:

₹200 per month for eligible elderly individuals aged 65 to 79 years.  
 ₹500 per month for eligible elderly individuals aged 80 years and above.

From the state government under the Shravanbal Seva State Pension Scheme:

₹1300 per month for eligible elderly individuals aged 65 to 79 years.  
 ₹1000 per month for eligible elderly individuals aged 80 years and above.

## Beneficiary:

All Categories

## Benefits:

Under this scheme, Rs. 1500 per month is given to each beneficiary.

## How To Apply

The applicant can apply at the Collector’s Office / Tehsildar’s Office, Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana Branch / Talathi Office.
