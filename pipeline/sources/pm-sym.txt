Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM) provides social security to unorganized sector workers in India, offering a minimum assured monthly pension of Rs 3,000 after the age of 60. Beneficiaries contribute a small monthly amount based on their age, with an equal matching contribution by the government.
Benefits Offer Under the Scheme
Assured minimum monthly pension of Rs 3,000 after attaining 60 years of age.
Government co-contributionequal to the subscriber's monthly contribution.
Family pension benefit: In case of the subscriber's death, the spouse receives 50% of the pension (Rs 1,500 per month).
Voluntary exit option: Subscriber can exit the scheme and receive their contribution with applicable interest/returns.
Spouse continuation: If the subscriber dies before 60, the spouse can continue the scheme by contributing regularly.
Life-long pension: Pension continues for the lifetime of the beneficiary after 60 years.
Affordable contributions: Low, age-based monthly contributions starting from as little as Rs 55 per month to Rs 200 per month
Eligibility:
Must be an unorganized sector worker (e.g., street vendors, construction workers, domestic workers, etc.).
Age should be between18 and 40 years at the time of enrollment.
Monthly income should be Rs 15,000 or less.
Must possess a valid Aadhaar number linked with a mobile number.
Should have anactive savings bank account or Jan Ohan account.
Must not be covered under any other statutory social security scheme (such as EPF0, ESIC,orNPS).
Must not be an income taxpayer.
Application Details:
Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or enrol online through authorized portals.
Provide Aadhaar number and savings bank/Jan Ohan account details.
Submit basic personal details such as name, age, address, and occupation.
Initial contribution amount is calculated automatically based onthe applicant's age.
Make the first contribution payment at the time of enrolment.
Set up auto-debit (monthly contribution) from the bank account.
Receive a PM-SYM card and account details after successful registration.
