The Taposili Bandhu Scheme provides financial assistance in the from of a monthly pension to elderly individuals belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC) community who are economically disadvantaged. lts objective is to ensure basic social security and improve the quality of life for marginalized senior citizens in the state.
Benefits Offer Under the Scheme
Provides a monthly pension of Rs 1 ,000 to eligible beneficiaries
Pension amount is directly transferred to the beneficiary's bank account (DBT)
Eligibility
Applicant must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
Must belongto the Scheduled Caste (SC) community
Must be 60 years of age or above
Should not be abeneficiary of any other government pension scheme
Must have a valid bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
Application Details
Collect the application formfrom BOO or Municipality office
Fill out the application form
Attach necessary documents (proof of age, caste certificate (SC), residence proof, and bank account details)
Submit the completed form to the concerned local authority
The application is verified by local authorities
Once approved, the beneficiary is enrolled and the pension amount is credited directly to their bank account
