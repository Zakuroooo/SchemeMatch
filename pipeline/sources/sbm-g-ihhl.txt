Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) - Individual Household Latrine (IHHL) focuses on promoting sanitation in rural areas by providing financial assistance and support to households for constructing their own toilets. It aims to eliminate open defecation, improve hygiene practices, and enhance the health and dignity of rural populations, especially women and children.
Benefits Offer Under the Scheme
Financial incentive (Rs 12,000 per household) to eligible rural households (primarily BPL and identified APL) for construction of an individual functional household latrine
Support for toilet construction materials and labor, ensuring basic, safe sanitation infrastructure
Convergence with other schemes (e.g., MGNREGS, SBM components) to support water availability, waste management, and sanitation sustainability.
Focus on behavior change (IEC activities) to promote sustained toilet use and improved hygiene practices.
Eligibility
Rural household without an Individual Household Latrine
Priority to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households as identified through official socio- economic surveys
Identified Above Poverty Line (APL) households belonging to vulnerable categories such as SC/ST, small and marginal farmers, landless laborers, and physically challenged persons
Must be a resident of a rural area covered under Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen)
Application Details
Identification of beneficiaries by the Gram Panchayat or local authorities based on eligibility criteria
Application submission through the local Gram Panchayat or relevant rural development office
Verification of eligibility by officials through field visits or existing socio-economic data Approval and sanction of incentive
Construction of the toilet by the beneficiary, either independently or with local support/assistance
Inspection after construction to ensure the toilet meets basic standards and is functional
Release of financial incentive (Rs 12,000) directly to the beneficiary's bank account
