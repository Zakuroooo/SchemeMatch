# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme

**Date :** 01/01/2000 - 

**Portal** : [https://nsap.nic.in/](https://nsap.nic.in/)

**Eligibility Criteria**

*   A Disable person in the age group of 18 to 79 years is eligible under this Scheme.
*   He / She is entitled to receive Rs. 300/- per month from Central Government under this Scheme.
*   He / She also receives Rs 1200/- per month under State sponsored Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana, so total financial assistance of Rs. 1500/- per month is provided.

## Beneficiary:

All Category Disable Persons

## Benefits:

Under this scheme, Rs. 1500 per month is given to each beneficiary.

## How To Apply

The applicant can apply at the Collector’s Office / Tehsildar’s Office, Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana Branch / Talathi Office.
