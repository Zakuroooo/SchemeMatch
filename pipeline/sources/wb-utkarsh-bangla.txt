Utkarsh Bangla is a flagship skill development scheme of the Government of West Bengal aimed at providing vocational training and employment-oriented skills to youth across the State. The scheme offers short-term and industry- relevant training in multiple trades to improve employability, support self- employment and create a skilled workforce. It is implemented through approved training providers, government institutions, private training centres and industry-linked partners.
Nodal Agency
Paschim Banga Society for Skill Development
Implementation
Training providers, Industrial Training Institutes, polytechnics, vocational training centres, industry partners, district administrations and local skill development centres.
Benefits Offered Under the Scheme
The key benefits offered under the scheme include:
Free skill training: Eligible candidates receive skill training in approved trades without paying training fees.
Industry-relevant courses: Training is provided in sectors such as construction, apparel, healthcare, IT-ITES, hospitality, retail, beauty and wellness, automobile, electronics, tourism, agriculture and other trades.
hort-term training: Courses are generally designed as short-term modules to help candidates quickly gain employable skills.
Certification: Candidates receive certification after successful completion of training and assessment.
Placement support: Training providers may assist candidates with job placement, interviews, industry linkage and employment opportunities.
Self-employment support: The scheme helps candidates gain skills that can support entrepreneurship and self-employment.
Training through approved centres: Training is provided through empanelled institutions, training centres and government-approved agencies.
Support for disadvantaged groups: The scheme encourages participation from women, minority communities, Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, persons with disabilities and economically weaker groups.
District-level access: Training opportunities are provided across districts to improve local access to skill development.
Improved employability: The scheme helps youth gain practical, market-oriented and job-ready skills
Eligibility
The scheme is primarily targeted at youth and job seekers of West Bengal. General eligibility includes:
Applicant should be a resident of West Bengal.
Applicant should generally be unemployed, underemployed, a school/college dropout, job seeker or person seeking ski upgradation.
Applicant should meet the minimum age requirement prescribed for the selected course (15 to 45 years)

Applicant should generally be unemployed, underemployed, a school/college dropout, job seeker or person seeking ski upgradation.
Applicant should meet the minimum age requirement prescribed for the selected course.

Women, economically weaker sections, minorities, Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and persons with disabilities may receive priority under inclusive skill development objectives.
Applicant should possess basic documents such as identity proof, address proof, educational certificate, bank details and photograph.
Application Details: Where and How to Apply, Process and Link
Applicants may apply or enquire through the following:
Where to Apply
Utkarsh Bangla official portal
Paschim Banga Society for Skill Development
District Ski Development Office
Block Development Office
Municipal office or local body office
Approved training centres
Industrial Training Institutes or polytechnics
Common Service Centres, where applicable
How to apply / process
The applicant should visit the Utkarsh Bangla portal or contact the nearest approved training centre, District Skill Development Office, BDO office or municipal office.
The applicant should check available courses, training centres, eligibility criteria and batch availability.
The applicant should register by providing personal details, address, educational qualification, preferred trade and contact details.
Required documents such as Aadhaar card, voter ID, residence proof, educational certificate, caste certificate if applicable, disability certificate if applicable, bank passbook and photograph may be submitted.
The training centre or concerned authority verifies the application and eligibility.
The applicant is enrolled in a suitable training batch based on eligibility, interest and seat availability.
The candidate attends classroom training, practical training and soft-skills sessions as per course structure.
After training, assessment is conducted by the approved assessment agency. Successful candidates receive certification.
Candidates may receive placement support, job fair access, interview facilitation or guidance for self-employment.
