The Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) is aimed at generating self-employment opportunities by supporting the establishment of micro-enterprises in rural and urban areas. It provides financial assistance in the form of subsidies and bank credit to entrepreneurs, particularly targeting unemployed youth, artisans, and traditional workers to promote inclusive economic growth.
Benefits Offer Under the Scheme
Subsidy (Margin Money Assistance):
15%-25% subsidy for general category beneficiaries
25% -35% subsidy for special categories (SC/ST/OBC/minorities/women/ex- servicemen/physically handicapped/NER, hi & border areas)
Collateral-free loans:
Loans up to 10 lakh (manufacturing) and 5 lakh (service sector) are generally collateral-free under CGTMSE coverage
Bank credit support:
Beneficiaries receive bank loans covering 90-95% of project cost (depending on category)
Own contribution (low margin requirement):
5% of project cost for special category beneficiaries
10% for general category beneficiaries
Maximum project cost limits:
Up to 50 lakh for manufacturing units
Up to 20 lakh for service/trading units
Entrepreneurship development training:
Mandatory training (EDP) provided to improve business and management skills
Eligibility:
Age requirement: Applicant must be at least 18 years old
Educational qualification: Minimum 8th pass required for projects costing above
Rs 10 lakh in manufacturing sector
Rs 5 lakh in service/business sector
Who can apply:
Individuals (including rural and urban unemployed youth)
Self-help groups (SHGs)
Institutions registered under Societies Registration Act
Production co-operative societies
Charitable trusts
New enterprises only: Scheme is applicable only for new projects; existing units are not eligible
Income criteria: No income ceiling for assistance
Eligible sectors: Only manufacturing and service sector projects are covered
Exclusion criteria:
Units already availing subsidy under other government schemes are not eligible
Institutions like private limited companies, partnership firms (in some cases), and large enterprises are generally not eligible
Priority categories: Special preference is given to SC/ST/OBC, minorities, women, ex- servicemen, differently-abled persons, and candidates from rural/hilly areas
Application Details:
Online registration:
Visit the official PMEGP e-portal
Fill out the online application form with personal, educational, and project details
Project proposal submission:
Upload a detailed project report (business plan) including cost, type of activity, and expected employment
Document upload:
Submit required documents such as Aadhaar, age proof, educational qualification, caste certificate (if applicable), and project report
Forwarding to implementing agency:
Application is forwarded to relevant agencies:
KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission)
KVIB (State Khadi and Village Industries Board)
DIC (District Industries Centre)
Scrutiny and interview:
Authorities review the application and may conduct an interview or screening process
