# Scheme for Providing Financial Assistance to Family Members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Victims of Atrocities

**Date :** 24/09/1997 - 

**Centrally Sponsored Scheme (50% State and 50% Central)**

**Government Resolutions:**

*   Government Resolution No. UTA-1095/Pr. No. 169/Mavak-2, dated 24.09.1997
*   Government Resolution No. UTA-2016/Pr. No. 298/Samasu, dated 23.12.2016

**Main Objective of the Scheme:**

 The scheme aims to provide financial assistance and rehabilitation to members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes who are victims of caste-based atrocities under the Civil Rights Protection Act-1955, practicing and encouraging untouchability is considered a crime and is punishable. To curb atrocities against Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act-1989 and the amended rules of 1995 have been implemented. 

**Key Conditions of the Scheme:**

*   The crime must be registered under the Civil Rights Protection Act-1955 and the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act-1989.
*   Medical certificate (required for relevant crimes)
*   Caste certificate (for all crimes)

**Benefits of the Scheme:**

*   Financial assistance ranging from ₹0.85 lakh to ₹8.25 lakh (depending on the severity of the case)
