West Bengal government provides scholarships under NSP WB for Minority Students to financially assist students from Classes IX to Ph.D belonging to minority communities
Benefits Offer Under the Service
Pre-Matric Scholarship (Classes IX-X):
Coverage of admission and tuition fees (primarily for Classes IX-X, as per scheme norms).
Monthly maintenance allowance for both day scholars and hostellers to support study-related expenses.
Post-Matric Scholarship (Class XI to higher education):
Reimbursement of admission and tuition fees (subject to course category and scheme limits).
Monthly maintenance allowance for day scholars and hostellers, with higher rates than pre-matric level.
Coverage for technical and professional courses, including additional approved fees (within prescribed ceilings).
Eligibility:
Applicant must belong to a recognized minority community (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, or Parsi)
Student should be studying in Classes IX to PhD level in a recognized school or institution
Must have secured at least 50% marks in the previous final examination
Annual family income should not exceed 1 lakhs for pre matric and 2 lakhs for post matric
The student should not be receiving any other scholarship
Application Details:
Apply online via NSP-WB portal
Register with basic details (name, mobile, email), EPIC, Aadhaar
Fill application form (personal, academic, bank details)
Upload documents (income, minority certificate, marksheet, bank proof, etc.)
Submit application before deadline
Institute verifies and forwards for further verification
Approved amount is credited via DBT to student's bank account
