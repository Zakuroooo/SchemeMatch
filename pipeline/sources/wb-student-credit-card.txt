The West Bengal Student Credit Card Scheme provides students with collateral-free loans of up to 10 lakh to support education from class X to post Doctorate in India or abroad. The scheme offers low interest rates and flexible repayment options, enabling financially disadvantaged students to pursue academic and professional courses.
Benefits Offer Under the Scheme
Collateral-free loan up to Rs 10 lakh for education from class X to Post Doctorate
Low interest rate (~4% simple interest per annum)
1% interest concession on timely full repayment inclusive of moratorium period
Repayment tenure of up to 15 years
Moratorium period: course duration+ 1year
No margin money required for loans up to Rs 4 lakh
Covers fees, hostel, books, laptop, and study materials expenses
Online application process for easy access and transparency
State government guarantee/support for wider accessibility
Eligibility
Must be a resident of West Bengal for ten years or more
Must be 40 years or below at the time of application
Must be enrolled in a recognized educational institution (India or abroad)
Should have completed at least Class IX (Madhyamik or equivalent)
Must have secured admission in an eligible course (school, UG, PG, or professional)
Co-borrower required (usually a parent/guardian)
Applicant or Co-borrower must not be in default of any bank loan
Only one student credit card per student is allowed
Must apply through the official online portal with valid documents
Application Details
Register on the official WBSCC online portal and receive an Application ID
Fill in the application form with personal,academic,and course details
Upload required documents (ID, admission proof, address, etc.)
Submit the application
Application is verified by institution and Higher Education Department
Upon approval, the application is forwarded to the bank
Bank conducts final verification and sanction of the loan
Loan is disbursed to the student's account/institution
