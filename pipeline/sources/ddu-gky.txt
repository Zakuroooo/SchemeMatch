DDU-GKY is a placement-linked skill development program launched in 2014 aimed at rural youth from poor families to enhance employability and provide sustainable livelihood opportunities.
Benefits Offered Under the Scheme
Free skill developmenttrainingforruralyouth
Placement guarantee after training
Focus on poor and marginalized communities
Residential training facilities
Career progression and support services
Soft skills, IT training, and personality development
Enhances rural livelihood opportunities
Eligibility
Rural youth aged 15-35 years
Belonging to poor households (BPL categories)
Priority to SC/ST, minorities, women, disabled
Must meet training eligibility criteria
Application Details
Register through state skill mission/training partner
Select training program
Attend skill training and assessment
Placement assistance after completion
