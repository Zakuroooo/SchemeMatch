Bina Mulya Samajik Suraksha Yojana provides free social security benefits such as insurance and financial support to economically weaker sections of society. It aims to reduce vulnerability by offering protection against risks like accidents, disability, or death, ensuring basic financial stability for beneficiaries and their families.
Benefits Offer Under the Scheme
Financial Assistance in case of death: Rs 50,000 (natural death), up to Rs 2,00,000 (accidental death)
Disability compensation: Rs 50,000 up to Rs 2,00,000
Provident fund: Full contribution paid by government (no worker payment)
Free coverage: Entire scheme is cost-free (premium paid by state)
Eligibility:
Age: 18 to 60 years
Residency: Must be a resident of West Bengal
Occupation: Must be an unorganized sector worker (including self-employed)
Category coverage: Worker should fall under list
Family income within Rs 6500 per month (except construction and transport workers)
Application Details:
Apply online/offline:
Online via the official BM-SSY portal
Offline through Regional Labour Offices and Labour Welfare Facilitation Centers at Blocks and Municipalities
Fill application form: Enter personal, occupational, and nominee details
Submit application: Upload/submit documents and complete registration
Verification: Authorities verify details and approve enrollment
Receive registration ID/card & SSIN: Use itto access scheme benefits
