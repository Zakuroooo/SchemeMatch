# National Family Benefit Scheme

**Date :** 01/01/2000 - 

**Portal** : [https://nsap.nic.in/](https://nsap.nic.in/)

**Eligibility Criteria**

*   Under this Scheme in case of accidental or natural death of a primary bread winner in the BPL Family in the age group of 18 to 59 years, the affected family is provided with one time lump sum financial assistance of Rs. 20,000/- is provided.

## Beneficiary:

All Categories

## Benefits:

One time lump sum financial assistance of Rs. 20,000/-

## How To Apply

The applicant can apply at the Collector’s Office / Tehsildar’s Office, Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana Branch / Talathi Office.
