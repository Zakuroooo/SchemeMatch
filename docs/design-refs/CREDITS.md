## Rules for people in photos
- These are real, identifiable people who never agreed to represent welfare recipients. **Never caption a photo as a user, beneficiary or testimonial** ("Sunita got ₹1,500"). Use them as context only, with no claims attached.
- Prefer photos where faces are not the subject (hands, backs, wide shots) for sensitive topics (widowhood, disability, poverty).
- Before launch, re-check that each photo is still live and still under the free licence; keep this table updated.
- No photos from social media, Google Images or news sites, ever (consent and copyright).
