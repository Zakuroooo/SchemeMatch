## Hand spot-check (raw answers read against their pages)
- **pmuy, A:** same rules as the answer key (woman, age 18+, 4 must-confirm items). The model wrote "attained 18" where the page has the typo "attained18"; the fuzzy quote check accepted it, as designed.
- **mh-sanjay-gandhi-niradhar, A:**
  - logically the same as the answer key, but it wrapped single rules in one-item groups;
  - the first scoring counted these as `wrong_grouping`, which was a **scorer bug**: a one-item group means the same as the item;
  - fixed before this report: trees are simplified before comparing, with tests;
  - "Transgender individuals" → `gender = other` is defensible; the answer key had left it as must-confirm.
- **nfbs, A:** read "aged 18 to 59" as the applicant's age, but the page means the **breadwinner who died**. A 60-year-old widow would be wrongly refused. The scorer files this as `ambiguous_source`, so the "excl. ambiguous_source" rows are **optimistic**: some of those are real meaning errors.
- **ignwps, B:** "widow" → `gender = female` (should be `marital_status = widow`); "BPL" → `annual_family_income lt 0`, which no one can meet. The validator should reject conditions nobody can satisfy.
- **wb-pre-matric, A:** applied the ₹2.5 lakh limit to OBC too. The page gives it for SC/ST only, so this is a real `wrong_grouping`.

## What the numbers say
- The large model (A) is clearly better: F1 0.87 vs 0.59 on schemes with a usable answer. It also produces the required JSON shape far more often.
- Groq "strict" mode **checks** the answer after generation; it does not force the shape. B failed 5 of 30 that way, mostly by closing the JSON before `unmodelled` and `ambiguous`; A failed 1.
- Recall over all 30 is held down by the 3 long PDFs that were never sent (too large for the free per-minute limit).

## What changed since the last run / what to try next
- First run: prompt v1, frozen before the run.
- Only the **scorer** was changed after looking: the one-item-group fix. It's a definition bug, not tuning, and it applies to both models alike.
- Next, one change at a time, judged on the held-out 10 once labelled:
  1. split long pages into sections so apy, pm-kisan and pmmvy fit;
  2. reject conditions nobody can satisfy (e.g. `income lt 0`);
  3. retry once when Groq rejects the JSON shape.
